Welcome to TrailBlazer!


TrailBlazer is a turn-based puzzle game where you guide robots through a series of rooms to unlock the exit and complete each level.


As you move around the room, every tile you leave behind turns into an arrow pointing in the direction you moved. These arrows create paths that the robots will follow once they start moving. You can change an arrow's direction simply by walking over that tile again.


Each robot has its own colour, and there are matching pressure plates scattered around the room. When a robot reaches a pressure plate, it stops there and activates it. Robots can also activate plates of different colours, so planning the order in which they move is important.


Your goal is to create a chain reaction. By guiding the first robot onto a pressure plate, you can trigger the next robot to start moving, which can then activate another plate, and so on.


Once all the required pressure plates have been activated, the exit unlocks. Reach the exit with your character to complete the level and move on to the next puzzle.


Good luck!
