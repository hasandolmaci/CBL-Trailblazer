Welcome to TrailBlazer!


TrailBlazer is a puzzle game where you set paths for robots in a series of rooms to unlock the exit and complete the levels. 


Every tile you move leaves amd arrow behind it in the direction you moved. The robots will then follow these paths once they are set in motion. You can always change the direction of an arrow by walking over that tile again.


Each robot is set in motion by the pressure plate with a matching colour. The exception is the blue robot which is activated by the button at the bottom of the screen. When a robot reaches a pressure plate, it stops and activates the next robot in a chain reaction.


Your goal is to create the correct chain reaction of robots so that every robot reaches a pressure plate. Only robots can activate pressure plates with the golden pressure plate being the last one activated.


Once all the pressure plates have been activated, the exit unlocks. Use your character to reach the exit and complete the level.


Good luck!
